# UI audit — 8 October 2026

The statically exported site was rendered in a local browser with representative catalog and session snapshots. No live travel searches or emails were sent. Pages were inspected at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844; the home page, privacy page, and waiting illustration were also checked at 320 × 700. The completed plan was inspected from heading through footer. None of these views had horizontal page overflow.

| Page | States inspected | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home `/` | Match and dates, travel preferences, your details; initial load and forward transitions | Form fields, buttons, and three-step progress fit mobile. The mobile progress labels now have separate columns. The first form section appears immediately; later sections retain the short reveal. |
| Plan `/plan/` | Connecting, preferences, searching, partial journey, ready beat, complete itinerary, failed, emailed | Cards and actions stay inside the mobile gutter. The completed itinerary now starts with the same spacing as the waiting and partial plans. The email action waits until the itinerary appears. |
| Privacy `/privacy/` | Static content | The card has the same mobile side gutter as the rest of the site, with space between its heading and body copy. |

## Animation state record

| State | Visible content and transition |
| --- | --- |
| Form initial | Match and dates is visible immediately. No entrance animation on first load. |
| Form step 1 → 2 | Travel preferences enters over 420 ms after an 80 ms delay, moving up 16 px and fading in. Heading receives focus. |
| Form step 2 → 3 and back | Your details, or the chosen previous section, uses the same entrance rule; entered values remain in the form. |
| Plan connecting | “Preparing your route…” and a pulsing red marker appear for the 400 ms anti-flash gate. |
| Plan preferences | A route card appears with “IN THE MAKING”. Its 3.5 s ticker cycles through “Checking your travel preferences…”, “Looking at your matchday plans…”, and “Putting your trip brief together…”. |
| Plan searching | The same route card cycles through “Comparing routes from Manchester…”, “Checking journey times…”, “Comparing places to stay…”, “Weighing price vs. journey time…”, and “Shortlisting your options…”. The example origin is Manchester. |
| Partial journey | A match card and “Finding your stay and comparing options” card appear above verified travel legs. The inline ticker cycles through stay checks, room and price comparison, and better journey options. |
| Ready beat | “Your route is ready.” and the “READY” badge appear for 450 ms after a previously visible waiting card receives an itinerary. The email action stays hidden for this beat. |
| Complete | The itinerary enters with a 350 ms fade and 4 px rise. The email action appears with the itinerary. |
| Failed or emailed | Terminal text and saved results or a new-brief link remain static. No rotating search copy is shown. |

The waiting illustration also has a 1.2 s pulse, 12 s moving route dash, and 2.5 s floating plane. Below 360 px, the arc and plane are hidden to give both city labels enough room. The stylesheet disables animations and transitions under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; this preference was checked in CSS rather than by changing the host OS setting.

## Changes from the audit

- Shared one top-spacing rule across waiting, partial, and completed plan content, replacing two separate declarations.
- Added a shared gap below waiting/partial cards before the session note.
- Removed unused secondary-button CSS.
- Kept the privacy card inside the page gutter and separated its heading from the body.
- Reflowed progress labels on mobile and gave the 320 px route illustration more room for city names.
- Prevented the email action from appearing during the brief ready animation.

The fixture snapshots exercise presentation and layout only. They do not establish live provider availability, price accuracy, or email delivery.
