# Mascot branding and domain shortlist

Research date: 7 September 2026. Open-web research used Exa; domain availability was checked in Namecheap's live Beast Mode search using Chrome. The original proposals below are retained as research history.

**Decision:** the user selected **Eagles Away** to support the wider Premier League ambition, purchased **eaglesaway.com**, and configured Resend with **send.eaglesaway.com**. Pete can remain the Palace-specific guide. App metadata, wordmark and email now use Eagles Away. The earlier Away with Pete recommendation below was superseded; the assistant did not purchase any domain.

## What Palace calls its mascots

Pete is correct: the club has **Pete and Alice the Eagles**. Palace's [May 2026 matchday information](https://www.cpfc.co.uk/news/women/matchday-information-palace-women-v-portsmouth/) describes them as club mascots meeting fans of all ages, and separately lists Phoenix, its live bald eagle. Pete is the appropriate character for this idea; Phoenix is a different bird.

The mascots already have an away-travel connection: Palace advertised appearances by Pete and Alice at its [official European final fanzone in Leipzig](https://www.cpfc.co.uk/news/information/where-to-go-in-leipzig-on-conference-league-final-matchday/). That supports the fit of a mascot travel companion, although the proposed AI personality below is our creative direction, not an existing club specification.

## Recommended direction

**Product: Away with Pete. Agent: Pete, your AI away-days guide.**

It feels personal, is easy to say and spell, and accommodates flights, trains and accommodation. It also survives a change of competition or expansion into domestic away trips. The tradeoff is that the name alone does not identify Palace: use a clear descriptor and club-approved visual identity alongside it.

Possible landing-page copy:

> **Away with Pete**
>
> Follow Palace. Plan the journey with Pete.
>
> Your AI guide to flights, trains and places to stay for European away days.

Possible opening message:

> I'm Pete, your AI away-days guide. Which Palace match are we heading to, and who's coming along?

Use a warm, lightly cheeky voice aimed at adult supporters as well as families. Pete should explain tradeoffs plainly and stay precise about dates, prices and missing information. Keep eagle puns occasional. Avoid claims about personal travel experiences or language implying trips or match tickets have been booked. The mascot adds familiarity; it does not change the planner's factual constraints.

Since CPFC is involved, ask the club contact for its preferred Pete artwork, name treatment and any existing character guidance, and align on presenting this as an AI version of Pete. There is no need to invent or infer those details from the costume. Alice can remain part of the club's broader identity without introducing a second agent or extra MVP behaviour.

## Domain recommendation

For an initial subdomain, use **pete.<existing-domain>**. The personal domain itself has not been specified; the product can still display Away with Pete independently of its hostname.

If adopting this name, buying **awaywithpete.co.uk** plus **awaywithpete.com** is a reasonable small expense: $17.96 combined advertised initial registration, before applicable fees/taxes, with currently listed combined renewals of $28.46/year. Use the .co.uk as the public address for the UK audience and redirect the .com. Either could also redirect to the initial personal-domain deployment while the hosting setup settles.

There is no need to pay for a premium name or an .ai suffix to communicate the AI feature. Keep the current implementation and main MVP plan unchanged until the user chooses a brand.
